# 4. Safety & caveats - just 2 people

- Never keep both passports, all the cash/cards and both phones in the same bag.
- On trains/coaches: lock the suitcase zips; valuables + DJI Osmo + passports in a personal bag, not on a distant rack if avoidable.
- London: put the phone away after checking the map; don't stand near the kerb holding a phone; take care at stations, Camden, South Bank and Borough Market.
- Highlands: stay with the coach, don't leave the tour on your own; be back on board on time at each stop; save the driver/guide number +44 7825 355358; bring a waterproof layer + power bank.
- Gatwick day: no extra sightseeing. Aim to be at LGW >= 3 hours before the 11:30 flight.
- Beijing: the city itinerary depends on temporary entry. If refused, stay at PEK; don't try to find a way out.
- Both of you save offline: the Leonardo Hotel Edinburgh Haymarket and London Airbnb addresses, flight/train numbers, bookings, emergency contacts and a meeting point in case you get separated.
