# 2. Before the trip

### 15 breakfast packs

✅ For 2 people in total: instant noodles/pho/congee/glass noodles, a mix of kinds; breakfast in the plan = £0.  
⚠️ Prefer dry, labelled packs; check ingredients at each border and avoid meat/dairy where restricted.

### Medicine

✅ Personal medicine + spares; pain/fever, allergy, digestion, oral rehydration salts, plasters/blister pads; motion sickness if you usually need it.  
⚠️ Essential medicine in the cabin bag; keep prescription medicine in its original packaging + prescription/doctor's letter if needed.

### Luggage locks

✅ 2-3 TSA locks + 1 small cable lock.  
⚠️ Lock zips on trains/coaches; passport, money, phone and DJI Osmo always on you.

### DJI Osmo

✅ Camera + batteries + memory cards + cable + case + lens cloth; back up video every evening.  
⚠️ Spare lithium batteries in the cabin; follow each attraction's filming/gimbal rules.

### Adapters/charging

✅ 2 Type G + 1 universal adapter for China + multi-port charger + spare cables + 1 power bank/person.  
⚠️ Adapters do not convert voltage; check devices are 100-240V; power banks within the airline's Wh limit.

### Clothes

✅ Waterproof jacket, warm layer, shoes with good grip, spare socks, poncho/small umbrella.  
⚠️ The Highlands in late October can be cold, wet, windy and dark early.

### Phone/data

✅ UK eSIM + a data plan for China + offline maps + booking screenshots on both phones.  
⚠️ Don't rely on a single app in China.
