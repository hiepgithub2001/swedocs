# 27/10 - Tower of London + Thames + Borough Market

💷 **Target total: ~£88/person.**

🌦️ **Forecast: rain 06:00-12:00 (~2 mm), then fair, clear evening, 12-16°C.** Sunrise 06:45, sunset 16:43, dark by ~17:20.

⏰ **Wake up 06:00, out by 07:30.** 45 min to get ready, 30 min breakfast; daylight is short (sunrise ~06:45, dark by ~17:20), so the early start goes into the morning.

### 06:00-06:45 · Wake up, shower, get ready

📍 Airbnb · 💷 £0

### 06:45-07:15 · Breakfast

📍 Self-made · 💷 £0

### 07:30-07:50 · Airbnb -> Bank / Monument

📍 Northern line Elephant & Castle -> Bank, direct ~5 min · 💷 Within the £8.90 cap

### 07:50-08:50 · Leadenhall Market + St Dunstan in the East

📍 Walk · 💷 £0  
💡 Leadenhall Market is a covered Victorian arcade (Diagon Alley in the first Harry Potter film) - dry in the morning rain and empty this early. St Dunstan in the East, a bombed-out church turned garden, opens around 08:00; 5 min from the Tower.

### 09:00-12:30 · Tower of London

📍 Walk · 💷 Joint ticket  
💡 It opens at 09:00 on Tuesdays - be at the gate then. Indoors while it rains: Crown Jewels first, then the White Tower. Walls, grounds and the Yeoman Warder tour after ~12:00 when the rain stops.

### 12:30-14:00 · Lunch

📍 Borough Market · 💷 ~£12  
💡 Walk across Tower Bridge, or go after the river tour, depending on the slot.

### 14:15-15:00 · Official Thames River Tour

📍 Boat · 💷 Tower+River joint ~£55.50 off-peak  
💡 40 min circular; the booking system assigns the slot.

### 15:15-16:45 · Tower Bridge exterior -> South Bank/Borough

📍 Walk · 💷 £0  
💡 Tower Bridge photos before the 16:43 sunset - the low sun is best from the south bank around 16:00-16:30. No need to buy Tower Bridge entry if tired.

### 17:00-18:30 · St Paul's exterior / Millennium Bridge or Tate Modern

📍 Walk/bus · 💷 £0  
💡 After dark: St Paul's is floodlit from the Millennium Bridge. Pick one branch; don't rush both (Tate Modern is free and open until 18:00).

### 18:30-20:00 · Dinner South Bank/Elephant & Castle

📍 Casual · 💷 ~£12

📍 Bus/tube · 💷 Within the cap
